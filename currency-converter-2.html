      About

      Rate is a simple, free currency converter built for travelers, shoppers, students, freelancers, and anyone sending money internationally. No account, no clutter — just a fast conversion and the tools to plan around it.

      Data sources

      Live conversion rates come from ExchangeRate-API. Historical charts use Frankfurter, which publishes European Central Bank reference rates for a smaller set of major currencies.

      Privacy

      Rate doesn't require an account and doesn't store your conversions on a server. Favorites, recent currencies, your theme choice, and checklist progress are saved only in your browser's local storage and never transmitted anywhere.

      Terms

      Rate is provided as-is for informational purposes. Exchange rates are not guaranteed to be current to the second and should not be relied on for financial, legal, or trading decisions. See the disclaimer in the Rate Information section above.
